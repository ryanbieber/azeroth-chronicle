## Scope boundary and perspective

Era 0 asks how Chronicle Volume 1 frames the primordial powers, the physical universe, world-souls, the Pantheon, and the Void Lords' Old God design. It ends as that design narrows toward Azeroth. Primordial Azeroth's loss of balancing Spirit, elemental wars, the Old Gods' arrival, and the Black Empire remain Era 1.

The slice does not attempt to settle later accounts involving the First Ones, Zereth realms, or different narrators' access to cosmic truth. Public copy says “Chronicle's account” where perspective matters. A later source that qualifies this account must become a separate disputed or superseding claim after review; it must not silently overwrite these records.

Sargeras, Mardum, demons, and the Burning Legion are intentionally outside this first Era 0 sequence. They form another Chapter I thread whose consequences enter the atlas much later. Adding them here without their own complete citations would weaken the prologue's clear causal line toward Azeroth.

## Implemented sequence

```text
primordial opposition of Light and Void
→ physical universe and Great Dark Beyond emerge
→ released energies form the Twisting Nether
→ six-force Chronicle framework establishes context
→ world-souls grow within some worlds
→ Aman’Thul awakens and searches for others
→ awakened titans gather as the Pantheon
→ Void Lords create and scatter the Old Gods
→ one threatened world, Azeroth, becomes the focus of Era 1
```

The Pantheon's formation follows from Aman’Thul's search and is stored as a causal relationship. The Pantheon and the scattering of the Old Gods are linked only by sequence, not by invented causality. The scattering of the Old Gods precedes their arrival on Azeroth.

## Visualization decision

Cosmic Origins uses a `relational` map-state presentation and `relational` spatial placements. Its original illustrated field provides atmosphere and visual continuity, while point anchors support story focus and dossier selection. These anchors communicate narrative sequence or association only. They do not claim canonical position, direction, scale, distance, borders, routes, or territorial control.

The permanent caption reads “RELATIONAL COSMOGRAPHY · NOT TO SPATIAL SCALE.” A plain interpretation note replaces a symbol-heavy map key. All ten placements use `geographicCertainty: unknown`, `placementKind: relational`, and an editor note. Schema validation rejects relational terrain displacement and rejects a relational placement without an interpretation note.

The cosmic field represents the abstract opposition, realms, forces, and sleeping-world imagery when its environmental composition already communicates the subject. Dedicated contextual visuals represent every key actor or embodied group: Aman’Thul remains a persistent clickable figure, while the Pantheon, Void Lords, and Old Gods appear during relevant story beats and are independently clickable for compact overviews. These are original project assets generated with OpenAI image generation from text-only prompts. No Chronicle art, game asset, logo, map, or source scan was supplied to or transformed by the generation process.
