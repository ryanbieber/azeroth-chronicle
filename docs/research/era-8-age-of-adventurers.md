# Era 8 Research Audit — The Age of Adventurers

Status: public `research` preview implemented; human lore, citation, chronology, cartography, interpretation, and visual review required before promotion.

## Scope and release boundary

The slice opens with the Ahn'Qiraj war effort and ends after the campaign on Argus breaks the Burning Legion's immediate crusade. It treats Outland, Northrend, the Cataclysm, Pandaria, alternate Draenor, the Broken Isles, and Argus as distinct chronological chapters within one top-level era. The Fourth War and all later cosmic developments belong to Era 9.

The compact MVP uses story nodes and explicit map-state changes as its secondary chapter pattern. It does not add a new state domain or expansion-specific engine branch. Outland, alternate Draenor, and Argus retain independent atlas coordinate systems; the guide never draws a geographic route between worlds.

## Guided-history spine

The ten-pane guide, **The Roads Open Before the Unnumbered**, follows:

1. The Ahn'Qiraj war effort and the rise of adventuring coalitions.
2. A separate-world passage into Outland.
3. The Northrend campaign and the fall of Arthas at Icecrown.
4. Deathwing's return and a separate post-Cataclysm terrain state.
5. Pandaria's return to wider history and the arrival of faction conflict.
6. The Siege of Orgrimmar and the end of Garrosh's rule.
7. The Iron Horde on an alternate Draenor worldspace.
8. The Legion's return through the Broken Isles and the class orders' response.
9. A separate Argus worldspace for the final Legion campaign.
10. Victory returning to a wounded Azeroth and handing the chronicle to Era 9.

The guide never launches battle playback. Each battle's five phases remain in its permanent dossier. Narration is original and paced for approximately 82 words per minute plus visual-settling time.

## Cartography and visual audit

- The opening, Northrend, Pandaria, and Broken Isles states use original post-Sundering terrain with chapter-specific geometry.
- The Cataclysm is a separate major-change state with original interpretive terrain and height art.
- Outland reuses its established separate worldspace; alternate Draenor reuses an intact-Draenor reconstruction in a distinct worldspace.
- Argus uses a new original terrain plate and independent `0..10000 × 0..6667` atlas coordinates.
- No cross-world route exists.
- Unknown actor locations use contextual relational anchors with editor notes.
- Anonymous adventurer and Iron Horde ensembles are interpretive group images, not named canonical gatherings.
- Existing figures are reused only where identity continuity is useful; Deathwing's event explicitly notes that the earlier Neltharion image is not an exact later appearance.
