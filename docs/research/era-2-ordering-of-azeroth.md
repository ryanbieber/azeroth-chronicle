# Era 2 Research Audit — Ordering of Azeroth

## Status and boundary

This era is implemented as a public research preview. It begins with the Black Empire state inherited from Era 1 and ends when the Pantheon departs, leaving the ordered world in the care of the keepers, titan-forged peoples, and world-forge safeguards. Later keeper conflicts, Galakrond, the Dragon Aspects, and the rise of ancient mortal civilizations are outside this slice.

The data follows the Chronicle Volume 1 ordering account. That account's narrator perspective and exact 2016 edition pagination still require human review. No record in this slice has been promoted beyond `contentStatus: research`.

## Causal spine

The guide and relationship graph state this sequence without treating every chronological adjacency as proven causation:

1. Aggramar discovers Azeroth and the Pantheon recognizes the danger to its world-soul.
2. Aman'Thul removes Y'Shaarj, destroying that Old God but leaving a catastrophic world-wound.
3. The Pantheon changes method: stone-and-metal titan-forged are created and elevated keepers command them.
4. The Black Empire falls; the remaining Old Gods are contained rather than torn from the world.
5. The wound is stabilized into the Well of Eternity and complementary world forges help hold the world.
6. Titan-forged works reshape the land and waters and foster living places across Kalimdor.
7. The Pantheon departs, leaving an ordered but guarded world to the history that follows.

Relationships typed `precedes` are intentionally weaker than `causes` or `enables`. The grouped war dossier is world-spanning and has unknown exact geography; it therefore has no invented path.

## Cartography decision

The tour uses two discrete states rather than a continent morph:

- `black-empire-map-research` is the inherited before-state.
- `ordering-of-azeroth-map-research` is an original interpretive after-state preserving broad landmass continuity while showing the central Well, more coherent waterways, mountain systems, living terrain, and subdued northern and southern facilities.

Ulduar is placed only at an inferred far-northern focus, the Forge of Origination at an inferred far-southern focus, and the Well at an approximate central focus. Cosmic observation, world-spanning command, containment, and confrontation use relational points, not geographic claims. The map calls itself interpretive before/after cartography and exposes certainty through text as well as styling.

## Visual representation audit

Aggramar, the titan-forged, and the keepers have original generated, clickable figures. Reused Aman'Thul, Pantheon, Old God, Y'Shaarj, and Elemental Lord representations retain their source-aware overviews. Contextual spatial states keep story-specific figures off the map until the relevant pane. The Black Empire, Azeroth's wound, the Well, ordering works, Ulduar, and the Forge of Origination are represented through terrain and accessible labels where those treatments communicate the subject more clearly than a person-like figure.

All generated image origins and transformations are recorded in `docs/audits/asset-origin.md`. These illustrations are interpretive research art, not claims about exact appearance or geographic scale.

## Guided-history and interaction audit

- Eleven unnumbered panes move continuously from inherited dominion to Pantheon departure.
- Every pane has an explicit map state, camera pose, highlighted subjects, deterministic actions, and 46–50 seconds of transcript time.
- Durations are budgeted around 82 spoken words per minute plus breathing room for a slow, weighty eventual performance. Voice recording remains deferred until all eras are stable.
- The main guide contains no battle playback or nested sub-story. The five campaign phases remain in the permanent conflict dossier.
- Clicking a visible figure or place opens a concise overview; permanent dossier routes retain full source and provenance detail.
